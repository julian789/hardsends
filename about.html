Why Hard Sends exists

Hard Sends started as a handful of spreadsheets a few of us used to plan finger-strength training. This site turns those spreadsheets into tools anyone can use — no login, no fuss, just numbers you can trust before your next session.

What we're building

Simple tools

Small, focused calculators instead of one bloated app.

Real numbers

Conversions and tests grounded in values climbers actually use.

Always improving

New tools and pages get added as the project grows — this page structure is built to make that easy.
