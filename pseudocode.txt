pseudocode:
julians climbing strength test:
choose bodyweight

crimping: choose edge size and kg lifted(if it was hanging, for 2 arm hanging do (amount of kg + bodyweight)/2 , for one arm do amount of kg + bodyweight. else if it was using edge lift, just take the kg lifted.) 
calculate total points for crimping with: y=(0.5^(x/5)), where x= edge size, y = factor. total points = (y*kg)/bodyweight.

pulling strength:
choose one arm or 2 arm, and pick reps and extra weight.
in a two-arm pull-up, each arm shares the total load moved (bodyweight + added weight): per-arm load = (bodyweight + added weight) / 2. In a one-arm pull-up, one arm carries the full load alone. Converting between them means doubling or halving that per-arm number.
calculate 1rm from x rep max with %1RM = 80 + 20 × 2^((1 - x reps) / 2)
total points = ((total weight lifted in estimated 1rm with 2 arms) / bodyweight)*10

core strength:
choose l sit or front lever ,and pick duration.
for l sit, every second = 1 point
for front lever every second = 10 points, however every second should be worth exponentialy less points after 3 seconds.
total points = amount of points/10

campus board strength:
choose rung number a and rung number b,
format: 1 to a to b
where a is some number between 1 and y
and b is some number between y and 11
and a is not 1, a is not b.
total points is determined using d1=a-1 , d2=b-a , total points = d1+d2 +min(d1,d2)
